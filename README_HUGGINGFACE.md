# ClipForge AI

Turn long videos (podcasts, lectures, interviews) into short viral vertical clips with word-level animated captions and AI reframing.

## Features
- **Speech-to-Text**: Whisper model with word-level timestamps.
- **AI Virality Scoring**: Detection of hook moments, punchy quotes, and storytelling segments.
- **Smart Reframing**: 9:16 vertical, 1:1 square, and 16:9 landscape reframing.
- **Karaoke Animated Captions**: ASS subtitle rendering with highlight colors.
- **Studio Clip Editor**: Interactive timeline trim, caption styler, and aspect switcher.
- **Export & Share**: 1080p MP4 download, SRT captions, and 7-day public preview links.
- **Strict Copyright Compliance**: 30-day retention cleanup and DMCA reporting portal.
